# Hop 2.4 adds an audio mixer and AI actions

The new Sound module gives each app its own output, volume and mute. For example, send Spotify to speakers and keep Telegram voice messages in your headphones. You can add an app before it starts playing.

The Input tab has microphone and selected-app settings for Hop Mix, with a separate level for each source. Enable Hop Mix explicitly, then select it as the microphone in your call or recording app. Closing Sound stops Hop Mix and microphone capture. App output settings stay active and return when Hop next launches.

- In the input list, pin sources, hide inactive ones and restore them through the Other Sources menu. The section starts collapsed; restoring a source does not activate it.
- Speaker and microphone icons in the menu bar show mixer state. You can hide them in settings.
- Sound has its own illustrated onboarding screen. The release card can show or hide the module without starting either mixer.
- AI can manage to-dos, timers, the stopwatch and time tracking through Hop’s local protocol. Each action requires confirmation through your client; Hop saves the result.
- Keep only the Hop icon in the menu bar, with indicators and countdowns hidden.
- When removing a torrent, remove its list entry, also delete its original .torrent file, or delete everything including downloaded files.
- Closing windows through the Dock keeps Hop running in the menu bar.
